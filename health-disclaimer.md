# Health and Safety Disclaimer

**Effective October 20, 2026**

**Talk to a doctor before you start.** Pipeline Prep gives general fitness training plans. It is not
medical advice and does not replace a doctor, physical therapist, or certified trainer. Get medical
clearance before starting this or any exercise program, especially if you have an injury, a medical
condition, are pregnant, or haven't trained in a while.

**You train at your own risk.** Running, swimming, underwater swimming, rucking, calisthenics, and
strength training carry real risk of injury, including serious injury or death. Underwater swimming
in particular can cause shallow-water blackout: never practice breath-hold or underwater swimming
alone, and only with a qualified lifeguard or trained partner watching.

**Stop if something is wrong.** Stop exercising right away and get medical help if you feel chest
pain, dizziness, faintness, unusual shortness of breath, or sharp or worsening pain.

**Rest is always an option.** Every workout in the app is optional. You decide what to do each day.
The app never requires you to train, and suggestions such as "Lighter" or "Recovery" are general
guidance only. If you're hurt, sick, or unsure, rest and talk to a professional.

**Health data is for information only.** Readings from Apple Health, Apple Watch, Garmin, or other
devices (heart rate, HRV, sleep) can be inaccurate. Readiness suggestions based on them are not a
diagnosis and are not a measure of whether it's safe for you to exercise.

**No guarantee of results.** Following the plan does not guarantee that you'll pass any fitness test,
qualify for any program, or be selected for any military, law enforcement, or first-responder role.

By tapping **I Agree**, you confirm that you have read this disclaimer, that you are 18 or older (or
have a parent's or guardian's permission), and that you accept the risks of training.
